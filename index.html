01 — Model

Physics that matches the machine.

Lumped-parameter models of cylinders, motors, electro-hydrostatic actuators, hydrostatic transmissions and proportional valves, identified from bench data, then ported from MATLAB to open-source Python so they run license-free on low-cost edge devices.

<1.3%NRMSE, Python port vs. MATLAB original
02 — Machine

Built from the ground up, then automated.

Power unit, proportional valves, cylinder, hydraulic motor and EHA, with pressure, position, torque and encoder sensors, data acquisition and edge computing, designed in Inventor and NX. Remote start-up, software speed control and fault-handled sequences now run unattended test campaigns.

<7%tracking error, twin vs. physical machine
03 — People

Operate it by hand, from mixed reality.

On HoloLens 2, the operator places a target by hand. FABRIK inverse kinematics and RRT* path planning turn it into collision-free joint commands, streamed over TCP/IP to the physical boom and swing, while a web dashboard shows every sensor live.

04 — AI In progress

Now, physics-informed AI.

An inverse physics-informed neural network for hydraulic state estimation: it learns from bench data while respecting the same hydraulic equations, extending the Kalman filter that already runs in real time on the edge.

Project notes

The details.

GPU physics engine · C++, OpenGL, GLSL

A real-time physics solver written in compute shaders: SPH fluids with more than 144,000 particles, XPBD constraints, and continuum-mechanics beams built on the Green strain tensor, all coupled to interactive 3D rendering.

Why it matters: the real-time counterpart to offline CFD, the kind of physics a twin has to run live.

Hydraulic component CFD in VR and MR · Ansys CFX, Unity

Gear-pump and pressure-relief-valve flow modeled in Ansys CFX, then rebuilt in Unity so the internal velocity and pressure fields can be explored in virtual and mixed reality.

Why it matters: it shows what no installed sensor can measure, in a form people can walk around and read.

Pneumatic digital twin

A hybrid physics and data-driven estimator (EKF, LuGre friction and Bayesian ridge regression) with mixed-reality control and an open dataset. Journal manuscript in revision, co-authored with two undergraduate researchers I mentored.

Why it matters: the proof of concept that scaled into the hydraulic crane platform.

Motion planning · FABRIK + RRT*

A FABRIK inverse-kinematics solver fused with RRT* path planning in Unity for collision-free control of a hydraulic manipulator.

Why it matters: the same planner now turns a hand gesture in mixed reality into joint commands for the physical crane.

Hydrocyclone separators · Ansys Fluent, CFD–DEM

Optimized hydrocyclone separators with coupled fluid–particle and Eulerian multiphase models, benchmarking RNG k-ε, RSM and LES turbulence models, with inputs validated by laser-diffraction particle sizing. Published in Fluids (2020).

Why it matters: where the model layer started, grounding predictions in the real feed, not assumed inputs.

My doctoral work is the hydraulic crane twin; in industry, I worked on human–robot collaboration at Hitachi America. I also teach and mentor: a smart-manufacturing course, two interns, and two undergraduate researchers who co-authored a journal manuscript.
